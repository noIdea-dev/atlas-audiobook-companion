Privacy Policy

Atlas Audiobook Companion · Last updated: September 29, 2026

Overview

Atlas Audiobook Companion ("the app") is a macOS utility that converts audio files and ebooks into M4B audiobooks. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data the app processes and how it is handled.

Data We Do Not Collect

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifying information. There are no user accounts, no login, no analytics, and no advertising.

Files You Import

Audio files (MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A, and others) and ebook files (ePub, PDF, MOBI) that you import are processed entirely on your Mac. The app reads the files you select and writes the resulting M4B audiobook to a location you choose. Your files are never uploaded to our servers.

Text-to-Speech Conversion

When you use the ebook-to-audiobook feature (AAC+), the text content of your ebook is transmitted over the internet to Microsoft's text-to-speech service for audio synthesis. The synthesised audio is returned to your Mac and the ebook text is not retained by us. Microsoft's handling of data submitted to their services is governed by Microsoft's Privacy Statement.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. The app does not handle or store payment information.

Media Library Access

The app may request access to your media library if you browse to a folder that contains your Apple Music library. This access is used only to let you browse and select audio files. The app does not read, modify, or export your music library.

Network Access

The app connects to the internet only to perform text-to-speech synthesis (AAC+ feature). No other outgoing connections are made.

Data Retention

The app stores only your project files and preferences locally on your Mac. No data is sent to or retained on any server operated by the developer.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised date.
